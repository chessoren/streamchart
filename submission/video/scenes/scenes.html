StreamChart

Every patient has a chart.
Now every stream does.
The problem

Citizen science gives a lot —
and never hears back.

01

No reply

People give an observation, and nothing ever comes back. So they stop coming.

02

No measured trust

Authorities can't trust data with no history and no stated confidence.

03

No continuous thread

Observations pile up without a story the city can act on.

OneAquaHealth built the organs of a health system for urban streams.
What was missing was the patient.
The insight

Medicine solved this a century ago.

A record that keeps the story.
A level of evidence for every fact.
A care loop: symptom, treatment, follow-up.
So we gave every stream
a medical chart.
“They gave a medical record to a river.”
